## What a reviewer should challenge

The strongest novelty challenge is that this is a straightforward application of known transport mathematics. Scientific value therefore rests on the resolved quantitative question, complete assumptions, source-specific interpretation and useful fixed-dose design consequences. Before any priority claim or submission, extend forward/citing-paper and supplementary-material searches and compare any exact annular calculation found. If an equivalent result exists, retain reproducibility value but withdraw or reformulate novelty claims.

The most informative new empirical measurement is a geometry-matched incremental insulin recovery experiment with time-resolved boundary collection and retained-inventory measurement. It should establish effective diffusivity, membrane resistance and source placement under the same conditions. Static one-hour secretion totals cannot identify all of those quantities separately.
